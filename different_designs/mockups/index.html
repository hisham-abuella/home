Five directions for hishamabuella.com

Each mockup is a working homepage with real content, in English and Egyptian Arabic (use the عربي button in each one). They all work on a phone and a laptop. Open one, try the interactive part, then tell me which to build, or which pieces to combine. The chosen directions now live in tracks/ and the gateway is the site's index.html; these mockups are kept as explorations.

Reader gateway

A landing page that asks who's visiting and sends each reader to a page built for them. Recruiters get a one-screen dossier, engineers get a terminal with research and papers, and learners get your journal of vibe coding and learning. On a phone each one switches to its own layout: big thumb buttons, a bottom tab bar, a docked action bar.

Five visual directions

1 · Oscilloscope

A dark instrument faceplate. The hero is a live two-channel scope. Choosing "hiring", "engineer" or "learning" changes the modulation on screen and the links beside it.

Strongest for: engineers and recruiters. It's the closest to the current site.

From Linear's "precision instrument" and Aceternity's wave backgrounds.

2 · Computation pad

Green engineering paper. The page is a worked problem: Given, Find, Solution, with pencil notes in the margin and every result boxed.

Strongest for: learners. It's warm and very readable.

From OpenAI's "lab notebook" and Perplexity's "scholar's parchment", with minimal.gallery's whitespace.

3 · Hardware catalogue

Aluminium and one signal orange. You're a product, HA-26: projects get model numbers, and a rotary knob routes each visitor.

Strongest for: standing out. It's the most memorable to a recruiter.

From teenage engineering's catalogue and Brex's "single ember".

4 · Constellation

A 16-QAM diagram where each point is a project. Drag the SNR slider and the noise locks onto clean symbols. Tap a point to see what it is.

Strongest for: all three readers. It teaches a wireless idea while it shows your work.

From Dala's "constellation on black" and Aceternity's stars and canvas reveal.

5 · Terminal on paper

A shell you can type into, or tap commands on a phone, above a typeset README. One typeface, black on white, and blue for anything you can click.

Strongest for: fellow vibe coders and engineers.

From Vercel's "typeset terminal on white paper" and vibeindex.dev's CLI culture.

Logo concepts

Six concepts generated on your Mac with Draw Things (Qwen-Image). The model garbled the Arabic in every one, so each keeps its generated mark and English wordmark, with هشام أبو العلا set on top in a real Arabic font as an SVG layer. Compare original and corrected. Concepts 7–9 were made as icons first, so they work as a favicon; the overlay page shows each at 16, 32, 48 and 180 px with the full name lockup.

Every mockup follows the same plan: a one-line positioning statement, a route for each kind of reader (hiring, engineering, learning), work ordered for recruiters, and a narration note on the writing. Judge the five directions on look and feel: their content was written before we settled on a journal-first personal site, so whichever one you pick gets the journal-first content and less Wireless 101. Article links only work inside the full site. The Arabic copy is Egyptian colloquial, and the articles themselves are still English-only.
