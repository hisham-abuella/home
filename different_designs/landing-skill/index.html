05

A short FAQ, in both languages

visiblemediumEN + AR copy

Plain questions people actually ask, near the bottom of home.html and ar/home.html. It helps search and AI answer engines (with a matching FAQPage block), and gives a learner an easy way in. Answers only use facts from the CV and the site. The Arabic is a first draft for the Arabic review.

What does a cellular systems engineer do?

I design how a phone's modem receives and cleans up a radio signal. At Apple I work on AGC (automatic gain control) for cellular modems, and before that on the 5G receive front-end and channel estimation at Qualcomm.

What do you build with Claude?

I love creating UIs with Claude. I build tools, sites and books, mostly in Claude Code sessions, and write build logs along the way.

I'm new to wireless. Where do I start?

Open the learner track. It's my journal of what I learned, entry by entry, with free resources.

How do I reach you?

Copy my email from any track, or open my CV.

What I'd leave out of the skill

  • Its fixed fonts, type scale, spacing and radius tokens. Each track has its own look.
  • No gradients, no italics, no one-sided borders. The tuner dial and the phone station bars use them on purpose.
  • The required "words light up on scroll" tagline. It's a template trick, and it would fight the tuner idea and reduced motion.
  • A single call to action on the gateway. Choosing a track is the whole point of the front page.
