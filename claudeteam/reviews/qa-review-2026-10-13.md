## Must-fix

### F1. The "Listen to this article" player shows on every article although no narration exists
- **Pages:** all 8 `Articles/*.html`, every skin and both viewports. This is also live at HEAD.
- **Steps:** open `Articles/journey.html`. `audio/articles/` contains no `.mp3` files.
- **Evidence:** `.listen` has `hidden=true` but computes to `display:flex`, and the `<audio>` is 40px
  tall and focusable. The player shows "0:00 / 0:00" and plays nothing.
  Screenshot: `listen-visible-without-mp3.png`.
- **Cause:** `css/article.css:348` `.listen { display: flex; … }` overrides the UA `[hidden]` rule.
- **Fix:** add `.listen[hidden] { display: none; }` in `css/article.css`, or a global `[hidden] { display: none !important; }` in `base.css`.

### F2. Horizontal scroll on `local-voice-generation.html` at 390px
- **Page/viewport:** `Articles/local-voice-generation.html`, 390×844, light and dark, every skin.
- **Evidence:** `scrollWidth - clientWidth` is 20px with no skin and with `?via=site`. It is 20px at
  HEAD too, so this part is already live. With the skins it becomes recruiter 8, learner 9,
  engineer 21 and **vibe 71px**, because the monospace font widens the table. The culprit is
  `table.model-table`, 392px wide (443px with the terminal skin) inside a 390px viewport.
  Screenshot: `overflow-model-table-vibe-390.png`.
- **Fix:** let the table scroll inside its own box at phone width, for example
  `@media (max-width:680px){ .article-content table { display:block; overflow-x:auto; } }` in `css/article.css`,
  or wrap the three `.model-table`s in a scroll container.

### F3. Engineer track (light): gold text falls below 4.5:1 on the gradient sky
- **Page:** `tracks/engineer.html`, light, EN and AR, both viewports.
- **Evidence:** `--star:#9A6412` measures 4.62:1 on `--sky #F3F6FF` but **4.09:1 on `--sky-2 #E1E8FF`**.
  The body background is a fixed radial gradient from sky-2 to sky. The pixel-sampled
  `h3` "Implementation" measured 4.47 at 390. Affected text: the section `h3`s ("Signal chain",
  "Implementation"…, 18px/600, so not "large"), `output#snrv` "22 dB" (14px), and the
  `span.code` labels "0101" (12px).
- **Fix:** darken the light-theme `--star` slightly. `#8A570C` gives 4.98:1 on sky-2 and keeps
  the hue. Or use a darker token for the gold text and keep `#9A6412` for the dots.

## Nice-to-have

- **N1. Gateway "Stay here" can't be reached by keyboard.** The first `keydown` (the Tab itself)
  stops the countdown, which hides the button. A keyboard user still stops the timer, so this
  works as designed. Tabbing reached 16 of 17 controls, and the 17th is that button.
- **N2. Gateway countdown under reduced motion.** About 90 rAF calls a second keep running for 30s,
  and the fill bar animates continuously (measured 180 rAF in 2s with `reducedMotion: 'reduce'`).
  The timer has a function, but under reduce it could tick once a second with `setTimeout` and
  step the bar or hide it.
- **N3. `grain-drift` keeps running under reduced motion** on `home.html` and the unskinned
  articles (`document.getAnimations()` shows `grain-drift`, iterations Infinity). This is already
  live at HEAD. `base.css` shortens durations to 0.01ms but leaves `infinite`, so the steps
  flip every frame. Fix: `.grain { animation: none; }` inside the reduce block.
- **N4. Vibe shell input has no focus ring:** `tracks/vibe.html:70` sets `.prompt input { outline: 0 }`.
  Only the caret shows focus. Fix: `.prompt:focus-within { box-shadow: inset 0 0 0 2px var(--blue); }`.
- **N5. Stale English live region:** if you open the gateway in EN and toggle to AR, `#timerLive`
  keeps "Opening the full site in 30 seconds" until the 10s announcement. Clear it on `langchange`.
- **N6. Article meta near-miss (already live at HEAD):** `--text-tertiary #6B7480` on Daylight measures
  4.54:1 on the token background, but 4.46–4.47 as rendered because the grain overlay darkens
  the background (`.article-meta span`, "6 min read", 1440 light).
- **N7. Skinned article with Contrast stored:** the reader pill shows no button pressed, and the
  header switch is hidden on skinned articles. The visitor sees Contrast with no control marked.
  Consider marking "Auto", or showing a small "Contrast" state in the pill.
- **N8. Track metadata:** all four `tracks/*.html` lack `meta description`, `og:*` and explicit
  favicon links. Browsers fall back to `/favicon.ico`, which works on the custom domain.
  A track link shared on LinkedIn would have no preview.
- **N9. Small tap targets:** "Open the full site" on the gateway is 18px tall at 390. Inline text
  links in vibe and learner are 19–22px. WCAG 2.5.8 exempts inline links, but the gateway link
  is a primary exit.
- **N10. Default appearance change (intended):** with no stored choice, `home.html` and the articles
  used to open in Midnight. They now follow the system (Auto), as the strategy says.
- **Informational:** no page has canonical, `og:url` or `hreflang`. `og:image` points to
  `https://www.hishamabuella.com/brand/og-image.png` (the CNAME), which returns 200 and is
  byte-identical to `brand/og-image.png`.

### 2. Contrast
I used two passes. The first composited the computed colours. The second, which is
authoritative, hid all text, took screenshots at every scroll position and compared each text
element's colour with the median background pixel in its box. Elements covered by fixed bars,
and audio fallback text, were excluded. The first pass flagged constellation and journal
articles, but those were false positives from the 1px star dots and grid lines in the skin
backgrounds. The pixel pass clears them. Skins, gateway, home and cv had no failures.
- Text over photos (excluded): `Articles/journey.html` `.hero-overlay .hero-title` and `.hero-caption`.
  The median ratio is 4.28–7.06 and the 5th-percentile worst is 2.88. The title is large (needs 3)
  and passes on the median. The caption passes 4.5 on the median at every size.
